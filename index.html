How to use ODO RUP

Shoe Deodorizer. Place one pouch in each shoe after wear. Recharge weekly in direct sunlight.

Car Deodorizer. Recharge weekly in direct sunlight in an open, well-ventilated space. Keep the pouch dry.

Cat Litter Deodorizer (200 g). Add one measured scoop per kilogram of clean litter and mix evenly. Remove waste and soiled clumps daily. If odour persists after scooping, add more deodorizer and mix gently. Replace cat litter regularly according to the litter manufacturer. Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place and away from children. Not for ingestion.
